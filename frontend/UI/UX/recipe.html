Tomato Egg Curry

20 Minutes ₹85

Ingredients

  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Onion
  • Spices

Ingredient Substitution

Butter → Olive Oil

Milk → Almond Milk

Price Breakdown

  • Tomatoes — ₹20
  • Eggs — ₹30
  • Onion — ₹15
  • Spices — ₹20

The Story

This comforting curry originated from a family recipe that transformed simple kitchen ingredients into a flavorful dinner loved across generations.

Cooking Steps

  1. Sauté onions.
  2. Add tomatoes.
  3. Mix spices.
  4. Cook eggs gently.
  5. Serve with rice.
